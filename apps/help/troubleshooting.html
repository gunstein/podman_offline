When something does not work

I cannot see my tasks

Check that you are signed in as the right user. Task lists are personal. Try reloading the Todo page.

Sign-in fails

Check your user name and password. Contact the people who run the service if you cannot get in. Never send your password when you ask for help.

A request failed

Reload the page. If your session has expired, sign in again. If the error persists, note the error code shown and what you were trying to do, and contact the people who run the service.

The browser warns about the certificate

Check that you are using the right address. Ask the operators to help you with the right CA certificate and setup. Do not turn off certificate checking.

Todo is unavailable

Check your network connection and try again. Working help pages do not necessarily mean that sign-in or task storage is available.
