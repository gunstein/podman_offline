Using the task list

Adding a task

  1. Sign in to Todo.
  2. Type a short task in the “New task” field, for example “Book a meeting”.
  3. Choose “Add” or press Enter. The task appears in the list.

The text can be up to 200 characters and cannot be empty.

Marking a task as done

Tick the box next to the task. Clear the tick to reopen the task. The change is saved immediately.

Deleting a task

Choose “Delete” next to the task you want to remove. The task is deleted immediately, without confirmation. There is no undo.

Finding your tasks later

Tasks are saved and shown again when you sign in as the same user. Editing the task text is not possible yet.
