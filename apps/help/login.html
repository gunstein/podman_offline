Signing in and out

  1. Open Todo and choose “Sign in with Duende”.
  2. Enter the user name and password you were given, and sign in.
  3. You are sent back to Todo and see your tasks.

Each user has their own task list. Other users cannot see or change your tasks.

Signing out

Choose “Sign out” in Todo when you are done, especially on a shared computer. The next sign-in asks for your user name and password again.

Forgot your password?

Contact the people who run the service. This simple app has no way to reset passwords yourself.
